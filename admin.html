🔑 Account Generator

Create a login for a client. Type their details, copy the generated line, and paste it into data/users.json, then commit on GitHub.

⚠️ Note the password somewhere safe — it is hashed here and can’t be recovered from the file. To reset it, just generate a new line and replace the old one.
💡 Easiest option: just message your assistant “add user <name>, <email>, <password>” and it will be created and published for you — no steps needed.
Or do it yourself:
1. Fill the fields above and click Generate.
2. Open data/users.json, paste the line into the list (add a comma between entries).
3. Save / commit on GitHub — the account is live in ~1 minute.
4. Give the client their email + password and the site link.
